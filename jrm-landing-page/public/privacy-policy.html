Privacy policy

Provisional version · 17 October 2026

Draft for the website testing stage. This policy needs to be completed and approved by the organisation before launch. Its registered identity, retention periods, hosting provider and international data transfer arrangements have not yet been confirmed.

Who is handling your enquiry?

This website presents Jesus Reigns Ministries Barcelona at C/ Riereta 4, Barcelona, 08001, Spain. The full legal name, registration or tax identifier and confirmed controller details remain to be supplied.

During testing, form messages are sent to alexbcn98@gmail.com. Use this address to ask about your message or its deletion. It is a temporary contact address, not a confirmed official mailbox of the organisation.

What the form sends

The form sends your name, email, phone number, message and consent selection. Its current configuration requires the contact fields. FormSubmit and the email provider process the submission; service providers may also receive technical request information. There is no application database for contact messages.

Why your information is used

The intended purpose is to read and respond to enquiries or requests for contact. This draft proposes consent as the basis for that use, through the checkbox beside the form. The organisation must confirm this basis and its handling procedures before launch. The form does not sign you up to a newsletter.

Please keep messages general and avoid medical details, other sensitive information or personal details about someone else. If a request involves sensitive information, contact us to agree an appropriate way to discuss it.

Services involved

Submitting the form sends its contents to FormSubmit and then to the Gmail test inbox. Read FormSubmit’s privacy terms and Google’s privacy policy. FormSubmit may use an anti-spam challenge.

The embedded Google Map and externally loaded Google Fonts also connect to Google services when loaded. Following social links takes you to the respective external platforms. Their own policies apply to those services.

The final hosting provider, provider agreements, processing locations and applicable safeguards for any transfers outside the European Economic Area remain to be reviewed and documented before launch. This draft does not confirm those safeguards.

Retention

Messages may remain in the recipient’s inbox and in provider systems. A retention and deletion schedule has not yet been approved; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion period. You can request deletion of your test message using the address above.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable, object to processing, or withdraw consent by emailing the contact above. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out. You may also raise a concern with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Updates

This draft will be updated when the organisation confirms its details and production services. Please review it before sending personal information.
